# Job Description

Northstar Labs is hiring a Senior Product Engineer to help build AI-assisted workflow tools for knowledge workers. The role combines product judgment, frontend architecture, API integration, and close collaboration with design and go-to-market teams.

Responsibilities include:

- Build polished product experiences with React, TypeScript, and modern web platform APIs.
- Prototype and evaluate AI-assisted workflows with clear success criteria.
- Partner with design and product leadership to turn ambiguous customer needs into shipped features.
- Improve reliability, observability, and maintainability across product surfaces.

Preferred background:

- Strong frontend engineering experience in production SaaS environments.
- Experience integrating LLMs or agentic systems into user-facing workflows.
- Clear written communication and comfort working with executives, designers, and engineers.
- Ability to balance speed, craft, and pragmatic technical decisions.
