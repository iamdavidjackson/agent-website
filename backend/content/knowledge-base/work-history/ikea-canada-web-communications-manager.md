# Responsibilities

- Developed web strategy to meet local and global objectives.
- Managed the bilingual IKEA.ca website.
- Provided management and guidance for the Webmaster role.
- Maintained relationships with numerous agencies and suppliers.
- Developed custom tools for IKEA.ca in Flash and JavaScript.
- Distributed email marketing materials and grew the recipient list.
- Assisted global partners with troubleshooting.

# Successes

- Managed and evolved IKEA.ca as a bilingual digital presence serving Canadian customers at scale.
- Built custom web tools in Flash and JavaScript to support digital marketing and product presentation.
- Grew the email marketing program recipient list over the course of the role.

# Lessons Learned

- Managing a large brand's digital presence requires balancing global standards with local market relevance.
- Strong supplier and agency relationships are critical when operating without a large in-house team.
