# Responsibilities

- Designed and built websites, apps, and digital experiences for clients with different business goals.
- Translated ambiguous business ideas into practical product requirements and implementation plans.
- Managed client communication, scoping, prioritization, and delivery from discovery through launch.
- Balanced technical execution with writing, storytelling, and presentation of value.

# Projects

- Built personal and client-facing web experiences that combined strong content structure with custom interaction design.
- Prototyped AI-assisted product ideas to explore automation, content generation, and conversational workflows.
- Created internal planning artifacts, schemas, and reusable documentation systems to accelerate future delivery.

# Feedback Recognition

- Clients consistently appreciated the ability to explain technical tradeoffs in plain language.
- Recognized for turning fuzzy ideas into shippable project plans without losing the original vision.

# Awards

- No formal awards recorded for this role.

# Lessons Learned

- Strong communication and expectation-setting are often more important than technical sophistication alone.
- Content structure matters as much as interface polish when the product needs to communicate trust and clarity.
- AI features are most useful when grounded in explicit workflows and carefully curated source material.

# Successes

- Delivered projects that helped clients present themselves more clearly and professionally online.
- Built a reusable planning and documentation process that reduced friction for future project definition.
- Developed a stronger ability to connect product thinking, writing, and engineering execution in one workflow.

# Failures

- Occasionally spent too long refining direction before narrowing scope to the smallest valuable deliverable.
- Underestimated how much structure a knowledge-heavy product needs before automation becomes reliable.
