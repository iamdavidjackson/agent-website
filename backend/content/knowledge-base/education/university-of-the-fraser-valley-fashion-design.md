# Description

A program in fashion design covering design principles, construction techniques, illustration, and drafting. Developed strong visual and spatial reasoning skills with a focus on bringing creative concepts to life through structured craft.

# Comments

The design foundation from this program — particularly the emphasis on visual communication, construction thinking, and making things look and feel right — has carried forward into frontend and UX work throughout my career.
