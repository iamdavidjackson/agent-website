# Description

An interest in how AI systems can be shaped into useful, trustworthy products instead of novelty demos.

# Why It Is Interesting

It sits at the intersection of strategy, interface design, system behavior, and human trust. The challenge is not just what the model can do, but how the experience makes that capability understandable and reliable.

# How Or Why Did You Start

This grew out of a long-standing interest in product clarity and communication, and became much stronger when LLM tooling made it possible to prototype assistant workflows directly.

# What Would You Love To Do With It

Design and ship AI products that help people think better, present themselves more clearly, and turn messy personal knowledge into usable systems.
